# Khaki Tours — Mumbai Heritage Walks & City Experiences
> Khaki Tours is Mumbai's premier heritage travel organisation, founded in 2015 by Bharat Gothoskar. Walks and tours are curated by local experts and hosted by passionate resident volunteers known as "Ambassadors of Mumbai".

## Flagship Scheduled Group Walking Tours
1. #FortWalk: Colonial Mumbai Heritage Walk
   - Price: INR 1,199 per person (All-inclusive)
   - Duration: 2.5 Hours
   - Meeting Point: The Asiatic Society Town Hall Steps, Horniman Circle, Fort
   - Key Stops: Horniman Circle, Bombay Stock Exchange, Kala Ghoda, Gateway of India, UNESCO Victorian Gothic ensemble.
2. #BandraWalk: Queen of Suburbs
   - Price: INR 1,199 per person
   - Duration: 2.5 Hours
   - Meeting Point: Bandra Fort Garden, Bandra West
   - Highlights: Portuguese Castella de Aguada, Mount Mary Basilica, historic gaothans (Ranwar/Chimbai), heritage crosses.
3. #BangangaWalk: Sacred Spring Heritage Walk
   - Price: INR 1,199 per person
   - Duration: 2.0 Hours
   - Meeting Point: Walkeshwar Bus Depot, Malabar Hill
   - Highlights: Ancient freshwater tank, Walkeshwar Temple complex, Rama & Lakshmana legends, samadhis.
4. #BazarWalk: Native Markets
   - Price: INR 1,199 per person
   - Duration: 2.0 Hours
   - Meeting Point: Crawford Market (Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Mandai)
   - Highlights: Crawford Market, Mangaldas cloth bazaar, Zaveri jewelry lanes, spice alleys.
5. #GrislyGirgaon: Tales of the West Coast
   - Price: INR 1,199 per person
   - Duration: 2.0 Hours
   - Meeting Point: Saifee Hospital, Charni Road
   - Highlights: 18th-century plague history, royal intrigues, burning grounds, ghost lore.
6. #FaithWalk: Multicultural Sanctuaries
   - Price: INR 1,199 per person
   - Duration: 2.0 Hours
   - Meeting Point: General Post Office (GPO), Fort
   - Highlights: Armenian Church, Parsi fire temples, Jewish synagogues, colonial cathedrals.
7. #ShimmeringCity: Night Architectural Safari
   - Price: INR 1,199 per person
   - Duration: 2.0 Hours
   - Meeting Point: Kala Ghoda Parking Lot, Fort
   - Format: Airconditioned SUV with moonroof for illuminated night viewing.
8. #HandmadeInIndia: Craft Traditions
   - Price: INR 699 per person
   - Duration: 1.5 Hours
   - Meeting Point: Swadesh Store, Eros Theatre, Churchgate
9. #Fort4Kids: Children's Heritage Treasure Trail
   - Price: INR 699 per person
   - Duration: 1.5–2.0 Hours
   - Meeting Point: Flora Fountain, Fort
10. #Bandra4Kids: Children's Suburban Village Discovery
    - Price: INR 699 per person
    - Duration: 1.5–2.0 Hours
    - Meeting Point: St. Andrew's Church, Hill Road, Bandra
